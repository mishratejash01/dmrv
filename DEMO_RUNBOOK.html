Before you walk in

Pre-demo checklist

Sixty seconds of setup buys a flawless demo. Tick every box.

  • Wake the app. Open dmrv-iota.vercel.app and sign in once — the first request after idle is the slow one. Get it out of the way.
  • Confirm the demo data. The registry shows the Deccan Biochar Cooperative project, batch PB-2026-01, and 36 credits. If you see those, you're loaded.
  • Start as the Project Developer. Signed in as developer@dmrv.demo (password AcresDemo!26), sitting on the Dashboard when the client walks in.
  • Three photos on the desktop for the live field capture — name them pyrolysis, flame, quench so you grab them fast.
  • Second tab parked on Traceability so the finale is instant.
  • Browser at ~115%, full-screen, bookmarks hidden. One-click demo sign-in cards are on /login — you'll switch roles from there.
  • Network check: load /registry-public. Credits visible = you're live.
  • Know your hero credit: RCC-BIO-IN-P001-2026-RMV-000002 — the one a buyer already retired. That's the finale.

The walkthrough

The step-by-step script

Scan the labels while you talk. The [SAY] block is your line — say it, then move.

1

Cold open — the whole operation, live

Beat 1 · Dashboard
Open
Logged in as Project Developer — you land on the Dashboard (the default screen).
Show
The KPI strip across the top, and the Sites & operations map beneath it.
Say

This is the entire cooperative in one view — every site, every kiln, every credit, read straight off the operational records.

Point out
The four tiles: Net CO₂ removed 36.4 tCO₂e, RCCs issued, Biochar produced 18.6 t, Buffer pool. On the right, open-batch meters already tracking the caps.
If asked
"Is this live?" → "One data layer. Every tile is a query against the same records an auditor sees — no separate reporting."
Next
Top-right avatar → Sign out, to switch into the field as the operator. →
2

The field capture — evidence at the source

Beat 2 · Field log
Open
On /login, click the Kiln Operator card. Sidebar → Field log.
Show
The Kiln run form, and the Required evidence card with its three photo slots.
Say

This is where it starts. Three photos are mandatory — clean pyrolysis, flame curtain, quench. No photos, no submission.

Point out
The right rail: Connection (Online/Offline), GPS auto-captured, Photos 0/3. It's offline-first — runs queue on the device and sync when signal returns.
If asked
"No signal in the field?" → point at the Connection badge: "Captured locally, synced automatically. Nothing lost."
Next
Add the three desktop photos → Submit for review. Then Sign out → back in as Project Developer. →
3

Review & sign-off — the separation is real

Beat 3 · Review queue
Open
Signed in as Project Developer. Sidebar → Review queue.
Show
The queue of submitted runs awaiting sign-off.
Say

The operator submits; someone else signs off. Producers and reviewers are separated — enforced in the database, not just hidden in the UI.

Point out
Approve / request changes / reject, each with a comment. An operator cannot approve their own run — the row simply isn't actionable for them.
If asked
"Can an operator edit after approval?" → "No. Approved records lock; corrections are appended, never overwritten."
Next
Sidebar → Production batches. →
4

The batch — and the limits the app enforces

Beat 4 · Batches
Open
Production batches list, then open PB-2026-01.
Show
The Tonnage and Age meters, then the composite sampling chain on the batch.
Say

Approved runs roll into a batch. Two live meters — tonnes toward 200, age toward six months. Amber near the cap, red past it. Whichever comes first.

Point out
PB-2026-01: 24 runs, 18.6 t dry, and the sampling chain — site pile → site sample → batch sample → accredited lab.
If asked
"What happens at the limit?" → "The batch stops taking runs and prompts closure. The rule holds itself."
Next
Sidebar → GHG quantification. →
5

The quantification — every input visible

Beat 5 · GHG
Open
GHG quantification → the Live GHG calculator. Select batch PB-2026-01 (it prefills the lab values).
Show
The breakdown panel on the right and the running Net CO₂ removed figure.
Say

Comparative LCA, ISO 14064-2. Not a number to trust — the whole equation, line by line, replayable by a VVB.

Point out
Dry mass → permanence from H/C 0.42 and soil temp → gross removal → minus baseline, project, transport → uncertainty → net. This batch's verified figure: 36.4 tCO₂e.
If asked
"What enforces permanence?" → change H/C to 0.80: the badge flips to Ineligible instantly. "The 0.7 threshold is live in the math." Then set it back to 0.42.
Next
Sidebar → End-use. →
6

Carbon locked — the end-use record

Beat 6 · End-use
Open
Sidebar → End-use.
Show
The map of application points and the proof photos attached to each record.
Say

The end-use point — biochar leaves the producer and goes into the soil. Quantity, method, recipient, GPS, photos. All on the record.

Point out
Four recipient groups, GPS-tagged, proof attached. This is where the carbon is durably stored — and it's evidenced, not asserted.
If asked
"Proof of end use?" → click a proof photo: the location and recipient are right there on it.
Next
Sign out → sign in as Verifier. Sidebar → Verification. →
7

The verification package

Beat 7 · Verification
Open
Signed in as Verifier. Verification → open the PB-2026-01 package.
Show
The single-screen package: Batch, GHG quantification, Laboratory results, Evidence chain counts, Findings.
Say

The verifier reads the whole evidence chain here — and can raise findings, but cannot alter a single source record. Independence, enforced.

Point out
Evidence chain: 24 runs · 72 run photos · 1 lab test. The H/C eligibility badge. Two findings raised and resolved. Then — Print package, top-right.
If asked
"Can she change the numbers?" → "Read-only on the evidence. She approves or rejects; she never edits."
Next
Sign out → sign in as Credit Registry. Sidebar → Carbon credits. →
8

Registry & issuance

Beat 8 · Registry
Open
Signed in as Credit Registry, on the Credit registry.
Show
The Issuances table, then the Credit ledger and the Serial number format legend.
Say

Verified and quantified — now it's credits. Two-person control: one admin initiates, a different one approves. You can't approve your own issuance.

Point out
PB-2026-01 issuance — Gross 36 · Buffer 1 · Net 35. The buffer took its 2% automatically. Every credit serialised: RCC-BIO-IN-P001-2026-RMV — project, geography, vintage, mechanism, all encoded.
If asked
"Double-counting?" → "Every serial is unique and retire/transfer-tracked. A retired credit can never be reissued."
Next
Sidebar → Traceability. This is the finale. →
9

The finale — trace it back to the flame

Beat 9 · Traceability
Open
Traceability. In the credit selector, pick RCC-BIO-IN-P001-2026-RMV-000002 — retired to EcoCorp Net-Zero Programme 2026.
Show
The numbered chain builds down the page: credit → issuance → verification → GHG → lab → batch → runs → feedstock → Field evidence.
Say

Here's the one that matters. A credit a buyer has already retired against their net-zero target — watch me walk it back.

Point out
Scroll to the Field evidence step and click one photo — the kiln burn that produced it, with the GPS and timestamp still on it.
Say — land it

From a credit on a corporate balance sheet, back to the photograph of the kiln, GPS and timestamp attached — in about ten seconds. Every credit you sell, defensible down to the flame.

If asked
"Does it run forward too?" → click Trace something else, pick a feedstock delivery → forward to the credits it produced.
Next
One short line on where we go next, then close. →
10

The roadmap beat — brief, and honest

Forward-looking
Show
Stay on screen. This is spoken, not clicked — say it and stop.
Say

Everything you saw is live today. Next, together: the system reads a lab PDF and pre-fills the carbon values for your reviewer to confirm, and flags any photo that doesn't look like a genuine run for a human to check. AI-assisted, human-confirmed — it makes the credits more defensible, never less.

Point out
Say the word roadmap out loud. One line, then move to the close. Never demo an unbuilt feature.
Next
Close. →

If something won't load

  • Stay calm — a live demo hiccup is normal, and composure reads as confidence.
  • Fall back to a screenshot of that screen, or open a neighbouring record (/runs, /registry) that's already cached.
  • Never improvise an unbuilt feature to fill the gap. If it's not on screen, it's roadmap — say so.
"The joy of a live system — one honest second while it loads. While it does, here's what you're about to see…"
"Let me show you the same thing from a record that's already open."
"I'll send a short screen recording of that exact flow right after this."
The close

Closing — the exact final lines

Deliver these, then stop talking and let them respond.

"You just watched one credit go from a flame in a field to a buyer's balance sheet — and come all the way back, defensible at every step. That's the platform, live today, with an AI roadmap that only makes it more defensible."

The ask — leave them with three moves

  1. A pilot on your data — your sites, kilns, and team in the system within a couple of weeks, so you're clicking your own records.
  2. Bring your VVB into a session — let them try to break the evidence chain. That's the best sales call we can have.
  3. A short proposal scoped to your credit volume, in your inbox this week.

"Can we get the pilot on the calendar?"
