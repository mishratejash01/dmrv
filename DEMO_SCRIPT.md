## 0. How to read this script

- **`[SAY]`** = talk-track you can say almost word-for-word.
- **`[CLICK]`** = what to do on screen at that moment.
- **`[SWITCH]`** = sign out and sign back in as a different role (proves the access model live).
- **`[BEAT]`** = a stage in the story.

### The one rule that wins this deal: **confident, specific, and true.**
We are not the loudest team — we are the team that *proves* everything. So we draw a hard,
honest line, out loud, between two things:

| Label | What it means | How to deliver it |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **LIVE TODAY** | The built app, doing the real thing on screen. | Total confidence. This is our proof. Click it. |
| 🔵 **ROADMAP** | Where we take this *together* — including the AI layer. | Paint it big and exciting — but always "here's the plan," never "here's what shipped." |

> The buyers in this market got burned by offset hype. **Under-claim and over-deliver.** Every
> time you say "that part is roadmap," you gain credibility, not lose it. Say it proudly.

### Demo-safety checklist
- **Use the seeded data.** Don't improvise new records mid-demo — the story below is known-good.
- **Never demo an unbuilt feature live.** The AI layer is *spoken*, not clicked. If asked "can you show it?" → "It's roadmap — here's exactly how it'll work," and describe it. That honesty is a feature.
- **Warm the app up** before the meeting (first request after idle is slower).
- **Backup screenshots**: keep stills of the Dashboard, the GHG breakdown, the Registry ledger, and the Traceability timeline-with-photo. If Wi-Fi dies, narrate over the screenshots — the story still lands.
- **Don't rabbit-hole.** If someone dives deep on one screen, say *"great question — let me park it for Q&A so we keep the thread,"* and move.
- **Two-person issuance nuance:** the system blocks approving your *own* initiated issuance. If you want to show a *live* issuance end-to-end you need two registry logins (`registry@` initiates, `admin@` approves) — otherwise just narrate it over the already-issued PB-2026-01. Recommended: narrate it; don't mint live.
- **GHG calculator number nuance:** the live calculator defaults the capture/processing/**transport emissions to zero**, so it reads a touch higher (~37–38 t) than the batch's *saved* verified figure of **36.4 t**, which includes haulage. That's expected — the **saved** quantification (shown on the batch, verification, registry and traceability screens) is the authoritative one. Don't read "36.4" off the calculator; use it to show the *transparency* and the *eligibility flip*, and cite 36.4 as the batch's verified number.
